Inspired by Cassidoo's [AI Usage page](https://cassidoo.co/ai/) I want to take a minute to promise that everything written on this website is actually written by me, not an [[AI]] or LLM. While I do use AI extensivly to write, edit, and refactor code I see that as fundamentally different from the (often painful) process of writing.

I write as a part of the process of understanding using AI to write for me would be antithetical to that. I am of the mind that "If you couldn't be bothered to write something, I won't be bothered to read it."

I have many thoughts around the use of AI as a __tool__, something that I constantly experiment with. While this is currently a stub I will likely come in and flesh out more thoughts below

### Other thoughts

- AI as a guide or questioner

In general for things outside of the technical I see the growing use of AI as something downstream of the loneliness epidemic
